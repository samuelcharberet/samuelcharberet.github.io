Metabolic rates have a long history of methodological development. In the late 19th century, Max Rubner had one dog spend 45 days in a large calorimeter to find out that the indirect and the direct methods for calorimetry gave similar values. Now, most researchers are using indirect calorimetry, which really should be called respirometry instead because it measures exchanges of gases like O$_2$ and CO$_2$.
