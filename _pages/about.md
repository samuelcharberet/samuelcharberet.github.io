Hi, I'm Sam. I am a biologist. I am driven by big questions. I often wonder if someday, we'll have a satisfactory definition of what a living organism is. Fow now, I also like to take care of aquariums and do maths for fun. I ended up working with animals only (penguins, moths, flies), but in a close universe, I am currently working on photosynthesis in unicellular algae, and in a neighbouring one, on the metabolism of archaea that absorb heat. I like models and data. Especially data that can be fitted by models, and models that can be tested by data.
