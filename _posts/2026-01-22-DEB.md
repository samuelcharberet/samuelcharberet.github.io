The Dynamic Energy Budget theory has been introduced by S.A.L.M. Kooijman in the 90'. This is a theory, which means it is a set of equations that are supposed to describe reality. Authors who started or contributed to this theory describe it as the best tested quantitative theory in biology {% cite marques_2018 kooijman_2009 --file others%}.
